How the show runs

The live vote split is the bar under each option on the big screen, filling up as answers come in. It never shows which answer is right. Worth knowing: people can see it while they vote, so a clear early lead pulls the undecided towards it. Turn it off for a round where you want independent answers.

The speed bonus adds up to half a question's points again, scaled by how much time was left. It rewards quick fingers as much as knowing the answer, so it suits a fast round and ruins a thoughtful one.
